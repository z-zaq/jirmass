About Jirmass Plastics

Specialized film extrusion and outer packing bag manufacturing for the pure water industry.

Dedicated to Pure Water Packaging Excellence

At Jirmass Plastics, we focus exclusively on solving the packaging challenges of sachet water factories. Water packaging requires zero failure rates: packaging film rolls must seal instantly and cleanly under high machine cycle speeds, and outer bagging sacks must never burst during loading and distribution.

We manufacture using 100% virgin food-grade low-density polyethylene (LDPE) to guarantee zero odor, complete compliance with food and water regulatory standards, and maximum tensile resilience.

💧

Plain Roll

Standard 320mm width plain LDPE rolls engineered with uniform gauge thickness (60–75 microns) to run jam-free through automatic packaging machines.

🖨️

Printed Film

High-resolution rotogravure multi-color printing reproducing your brand identity, batch numbers, and regulatory certification seals with sharp clarity.

🛍️

Printed Packing Bag

Puncture-resistant outer bundling bags with custom factory branding designed to carry 20 water sachets through rigorous warehouse transit.

📦

Shrink Wrap / SSB & MSB

Heavy-duty thermal shrink wrap calibrated for Single Sachet Bag (SSB) & Multiple Sachet Bag (MSB) collation and bottle multipacks.

Ready to Supply Your Pure Water Factory?

Whether you need Plain Roll supplies, custom Printed Film, bulk Printed Packing Bags, or Shrink Wrap / SSB & MSB, our production lines are built to fulfill your schedule.
